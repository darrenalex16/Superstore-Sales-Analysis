# Superstore Sales and Profitability Analysis

Tableau and Excel analysis of 51,228 sales records across 7 global markets, covering 2020–2023.

This academic project examines sales performance, profitability, shipping costs, and discount patterns, with a focus on the Asia-Pacific (APAC) market. Five Tableau dashboards present the findings and support business recommendations.

**Author:** Darren Alexander  
**Institution:** University of London, SIM Global Education  
**Course:** Business Analytics, Applied Modelling and Prediction  
**Project period:** January–March 2025  

## Project Objectives

- Compare sales and profitability across global markets.
- Identify leading countries and product categories within APAC.
- Compare shipping costs relative to sales.
- Explore the relationship between discounts and sales.
- Produce sales and profit forecasts to support planning.

## Key Findings

### 1. APAC led global sales and profit

APAC generated approximately **$3.58 million in sales** and **$436,586 in profit**, the highest totals among the seven markets.

### 2. Three countries led APAC sales

| Country | Total Sales |
|---|---:|
| Australia | $925,236 |
| China | $699,904 |
| India | $588,639 |

These countries provided a focus for deeper analysis of product performance, shipping costs, and trends.

### 3. Technology was APAC's most profitable category

| Product Category | Total Profit |
|---|---:|
| Technology | $204,410 |
| Furniture | $126,823 |
| Office Supplies | $105,353 |

Technology contributed approximately **46.8% of APAC profit**, making it a priority for further product and investment analysis.

### 4. High sales did not always mean the highest profit margin

Malaysia, Cambodia, and Hong Kong recorded profit margins of approximately **26.61%**, **25.62%**, and **25.35%**, respectively.

Comparing sales with profit margins provides a fuller picture of market performance than sales rankings alone.

### 5. Forecasts supported forward planning

The project forecast approximately **$387,170 in APAC sales** and **$45,911 in APAC profit** for Q4 2024.

These figures are model projections produced for the coursework, not observed results or guaranteed outcomes.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritise further analysis of Technology products because of their contribution to APAC profit.
- Assess growth opportunities using sales volume, profit margins, and market size together.
- Investigate product mix, discounting, and operating costs in markets with negative margins.
- Monitor shipping costs as a percentage of sales to identify potential efficiency improvements.
- Test promotional changes against profit outcomes before expanding discounts.
- Use forecasts as planning inputs and compare them with actual results when available.

These recommendations were proposed as part of an academic project. No implemented business impact is claimed.

## Notes and Limitations

- This is an academic analysis of the supplied Global Superstore dataset.
- Historical dates refer to the dates recorded in the included Excel file.
- Financial totals in this README were checked against the included dataset.
- Profit margin is calculated as total profit divided by total sales.
- Observed relationships do not establish causation.
- Forecast accuracy was not independently validated for this README.
